# 🩺MedBox – Keep Your Medical World Organized

**📌Project Type:** UI/UX Design Project (Basic)

A healthcare report organizer concept designed to help individuals and families store, manage, and easily access their medical records in one place.

## 📖Project Overview

MedBox is a UI/UX design project focused on making medical report management simpler and more organised. It helps users keep their medical records in one place instead of searching through scattered phone galleries, PDFs, emails, and physical files.

The concept focuses on easy navigation, organised records, and quick access to medical reports.

## ⚠️Problem Statement

People often store their medical reports in different places, such as phone galleries, physical files, and digital folders. This makes it difficult to keep track of records and find the right report whenever needed.

## 🎯Project Goal

To design a simple and user-friendly experience that allows individuals and families to organise, store, and access their medical reports conveniently.

## ✨Key Features

- Add and manage medical reports
- Four report categories
- Separate healthcare profiles
- Search by report name and date
- Recently opened reports
- Report Preview and sharing
